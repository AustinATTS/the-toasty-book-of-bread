# Connections: neighbouring differences, distant likenesses

Connections: neighbouring differences, distant likenesses is a standalone excerpt from The Toasty
Book of Bread: An Illustrated Guide to the World's Greatest Loaves by Austin Welsh-Graham, part of
the Toasty Panetteria book series.

These six illustrated essays explore bread through the work it does. They follow neighbouring breads
whose names conceal different preparations, distant flatbreads whose shared shapes lead to different
meals, rings and the water before baking, living cultures, breads made to keep, and the ways bread
carries or gathers food.

The essays are The bread next door, A circle becomes a sheet, A ring and the water before fire, What
the bowl remembers, Bread for tomorrow, and The meal in the bread. Histories, makers' accounts and
kitchen observations guide the comparisons. Shared shape is treated as a starting point for
curiosity rather than proof of common ancestry.

This deposit contains only the Connections opening and six essays, with their illustrations and
cited references. It does not include the full book, regional bread chapters or recipes. References
to pages and recipes outside the excerpt refer to the complete book. The excerpt is drawn from
first-edition production revision 1.0.

The full book explores bread across Europe, Africa, Asia, the Americas and Oceania through
forty-eight principal entries and fifty-one author-tested recipes. This excerpt offers a focused
route through its central theme: neighbouring differences and distant likenesses.

Author: Austin Welsh-Graham, the software developer and baker behind Toasty Panetteria. Author
