\section*{About this excerpt}
This publication shares the Connections opening and six essays from \emph{The Toasty Book of Bread: An Illustrated Guide to the World's Greatest Loaves}. The regional bread entries and recipes remain in the complete book. The essays' comparative illustrations and all sources they cite are included here.

Page references to bread entries, recipes and kitchen guidance refer to the printed page numbers in the complete book, revision 1.0. They are invitations to continue reading there, rather than links to missing pages in this excerpt. The references at the end belong to the essays and use their own citation numbering.

The parent book belongs to the Toasty Panetteria book series. Its paperback ISBN is 979-8-1852-5111-9 and its hardback ISBN is 979-8-1616-3430-1. These ISBNs identify the complete print editions, not this excerpt.
